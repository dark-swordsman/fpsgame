# World & Environment

## Setting

- Eastern European aesthetic — Chernarus / STALKER-style terrain and architecture
- Large open world map (Chernarus scale or larger)
- Coastal town(s), forested hills, open fields, military zones

## Atmosphere

- Primarily depressing weather — overcast, rain, fog, snow
- Day/night cycle — night is a gameplay element, not just visual
- Dynamic seasons

## World Features

- Anomalies — environmental hazards with STALKER-style feel; rarer, tied to events or specific zones
- Sound propagation — gunshots, footsteps, and anomaly sounds carry through the environment and trigger AI reactions
- Foliage, lighting, and weather all contribute to the north star scene feel

## Sound Design

Sound is a first-class gameplay mechanic:
- Footsteps on different surfaces (wood, gravel, mud, water)
- Ambient environmental sounds (wind, wildlife, distant town sounds)
- AI reacts to sound — sound is both a threat and a tool
- Hunt: Showdown is a reference for how sound awareness shapes player behavior

## Notes

The north star scene is the target:
> Night, hill sloping west, treeline to the left, coastal town below, bunker entrance ahead. Monsters in the dark responding to sounds.

Every environment feature should be evaluated against this image.
