## Attachments

Rail systems: Dovetail, Picatinny
Attachment types (TBD — suppressors, optics, grips, barrels at minimum)

- Barrel length: longer = higher velocity, more weight, worse ergonomics, larger physical footprint (harder in tight spaces); shorter = lower velocity, lighter, faster handling, better CQB
- Suppressors: slightly increase velocity (barrel extension effect), reduce sound signature, increase jam chance (backpressure/gassing) — modeled closer to real life
