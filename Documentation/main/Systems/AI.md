# AI

## Enemy Types

- Zombies / infected — common threat
- Anomaly-type creatures — rarer, better suited to events or specific zones
- Potential for human AI (bandits, traders, factions) — TBD

## Behavior

- Sound-reactive — enemies respond to player and environmental sounds
- Sound as a core gameplay mechanic: footsteps, gunshots, and environmental noise attract or alert AI
- AI behavior tied to environment (see [[Environment|World & Environment]])

## Notes

- Complex AI interactions are a core pillar — not just "shoot at player"
- AI should make the north star scene feel alive (things crawling in the dark, reacting to noise)
