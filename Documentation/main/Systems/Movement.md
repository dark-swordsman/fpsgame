# Movement

## Feel

- Snappy, not floaty
- Quick turnaround speed
- Walking backward at ~50-60% of forward speed
- Reference: Tarkov's inertia system is a good baseline — both pre- and post-inertia Tarkov are valid reference points
- Avoid: old CSGO floatiness

## Notes

- Movement should complement gunplay — snappy repositioning without arcade feel
- Sound from movement is a gameplay element (footsteps, debris) — see [[World & Environment]]
