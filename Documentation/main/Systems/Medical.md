# Medical

## References

- **ACES (ARMA 3)** — depth and UX model; very in-depth, we won't go that far but it's the target ceiling
- **Tarkov** — ballistics interaction with body health (limb damage, fractures, bleed, etc.)

## UX Model (from ACES)

- Hotkeys to initiate treatment actions
- Visual drag/selection UI for choosing treatment options
- Context-sensitive: what's available depends on which body part the player is looking at
- This interaction pattern is worth carrying forward even if the underlying simulation depth is reduced

## Depth

- Base game will be simpler than ACES or Tarkov; exact depth TBD through iteration
- Body part damage: in scope, granularity TBD
- Healing items: bandages, medkits, etc. at minimum

## Goals

- Survival tension without micromanagement
- Medical decisions should feel meaningful, not tedious
